Rules in place
Actions:
Spawn, move, heal, attack, capture settlement/city, upgrade/downgrade soldier, unstation/destation cannon, sneak
Capturing a settlement/city also changes the color of the roads around it, chain by chain. A chain is a run of road between buildings, junctions (3+ roads meeting at an empty intersection) and dead ends. Every chain in the captured building's road network turns your color — whoever owned it, walls included — unless one of its ends is another player's settlement/city; that chain keeps its owners. A fork is judged branch by branch.
While a battle or a wall crossing is in progress, nobody can move, attack, capture, heal, fire, sail, board or build until it resolves.
Clockwise to persons turn
Knocked out: a player with no settlements, no cities, and no alive soldiers (injured soldiers count as alive) is knocked out — they get no more turns and can't act, but can still spectate. The last player standing wins.
Wheat can be played for an extra movement
Specific Battle rules
When attacking, you choose how many soldiers to attack with. Defender defend with whatever is on the defending turf. The attacker can continue attack as long as they have soldier but soldier amount is locked.
When battling in turf with a mix between injured and full health soldiers, only the full health soldiers fight. Once they are all gone the battle ends — the injured defenders do not fight on and stay injured. Attacking them is a separate fight that needs a new action.
When cannon splash attack on own tile, canon itself is immune but ally troops can still get hit
When mix fight with cannon in turf, cannon is lowest roll
Reroll if tie on ambiguous turf
Attacker
After a battle, the attacker gets to move their injured soldiers away first (before the defender repositions theirs)
If the attacker ends a battle early while both sides still have troops, the attacker flees and the battle counts as a defender win (it feeds the Conqueror bonus).


Robber
Soldiers can fight robber off of resources by doing a standard fight. If they win, they take from pile and move robber 1 tile away of their choosing
When discarding, choose half of discarded cards to put into the robber pile, face down.
If rolled on robber tile, robber gets that card face up.
Smuggle: pay robber and card to take soldier to its location
Robber wins ties
Rolling a 7: every player holding 8 or more resource cards must discard half their hand (rounded down), choosing which resource cards to lose. All discards must be resolved before the robber is moved, then the steal happens.
Robber bag: every resource card discarded by the 7 rule, plus the resources produced by a hex the robber sits on, go into the robber's bag (shown below the dice in the sidebar).

Walls 
Upgrade one of your roads to a wall during Build with 2 bricks. The road flips 90 degrees.
Walls block enemy soldiers only — your own troops cross freely. An enemy soldier crossing opens the wall gate: the crosser picks a number 1-6, then the wall owner rolls. Whoever doesn't act within 30s (or is offline) has their number picked for them. Match = the soldier crosses; miss = the soldier stays and its action is spent. Ex. person A's soldier picks 5 crossing person B's wall; B rolls 5, the soldier crosses.
A wall changes hands on capture exactly like the road it sits on (see capture above).
A stationed cannon adjacent to a wall can attack it: roll 4+ breaks the wall back into a normal road (the road survives).





Cannon
Upgrade a healthy soldier to a cannon on your settlement/city with 1 rock and 1 wood (an injured soldier must heal first). Starts unstationed. Downgrading back to soldier costs your action and works anywhere.
Actions: ranged attack (direction aim, with an optional chain-shot toggle), wall attack, station, unstation, move — each costs the cannon's one action per phase.
Can only move while unstationed. Can only attack while stationed.
Ranged attack: stationed, aim by direction — Fire opens a compass chip per adjacent vertex holding a valid target; a vertex with more than one target asks you to pick the soldier. The cannon and the target each roll; win = margin rules on the target (>=2 kills, 1 injures; a target cannon dies on any loss), ties go to the cannon, lose = nothing happens. Everyone sees the resolved shot briefly.
Chain shot (toggle inside Fire, replaces the old Splash attack): pay 1 rock to load. Aim at a vertex one intersection away or the cannon's own vertex. Firing with it on hits the whole vertex — the cannon rolls once against every soldier there, even your own — resolving each pair independently. The cannon itself is immune; losing rolls do nothing to it.
Wall attack: stationed next to a wall, roll 4+ to break it back into a road.
Shots are final: a ranged attack, chain shot or wall attack can't be undone, and neither can anything you did before it that phase.
Melee: a cannon in a normal battle always pairs as its side's lowest roll — it faces the enemy's weakest die. It wins its ties (no home field advantage) and dies on ANY lost roll — cannons are never injured.
On a ship: a cannon can station aboard and fire at targets 1 intersection from the ship's vertex (naval artillery).

Resource mines
Randomly place a few huts(monopoly houses) in the middle of hexes (perferably one on each resource). You can claim mines by having a soldier go into it. When claimed, the resource of that hex is claimed when dice rolls on it. Cannons can not hold a mine.
Soldiers can get off at any road but not buildings
No cannons
Counts as one victory point
Ships
Build with 1 wood 1 sheep during Build: click a coast vertex your road or settlement touches — the ship spawns on that vertex. Ships sit on coast and sea vertices, and may dock over a settlement.
Can transport any number of troops. Boarding and landing each cost the soldier's action and happen only on the ship's own coast vertex — a ship in open sea can't load or unload.
Wind Movement: each ship moves ceil(this turn's dice roll / 2) during each player's own Action phase — a rolled 7 still gives 4. Moving the ship doesn't use the troops' actions.
An empty ship belongs to whoever sails or boards it. A ship with troops aboard answers only to its owner, and other players can't board it.
Troops aboard can be attacked like any troops on that vertex, but they must land before they can capture or start a fight.
If cannon aboard, it can attack. Station rules apply.
A Knight spawned where your troops are all aboard a ship joins them aboard.
Can be turned into a settlement on its own or an adjacent coast vertex with 1 wheat and 1 ore. The distance rule applies. No free garrison soldier. A settlement can't be built under a ship.
The Admiral: own the largest fleet (at least 3 ships, strictly more than every rival — the holder keeps it on a tie). Worth 1 victory point, and each ship the holder owns gets +1 movement per Action phase.
